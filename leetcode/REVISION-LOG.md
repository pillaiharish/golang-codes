## After an unsuccessful attempt

1. Label the issue **pattern recognition**, **invariant**, **Go syntax**, **data structure**, **edge case**, or **complexity/proof**.
2. Read just that subsection of the problem and its shared building block.
3. Close the notes and rewrite the explanation/code from scratch.
4. Re-test on a different input and record a new attempt above. Do not erase the failure.
