## Before each new interview

- [ ] Scan the module dashboard and the last review entries.
- [ ] Identify the three topics with the lowest confidence or most recent misses.
- [ ] Give a 60-second answer per topic without opening notes.
- [ ] Re-run one relevant coding/race/benchmark exercise.
- [ ] Revisit the bounded publisher's failure cases and explain what was actually verified.
- [ ] Capture remaining doubts and next-revision dates.

## Suggested spaced review cadence

Treat these as reminders, not claims of learning: revisit hard material after **1 day**, **3 days**, **7 days**, then **14–30 days**, adjusting based on failed recall. Update the next review column according to actual confidence.
