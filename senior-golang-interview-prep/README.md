# Senior Go Engineering — Interview Preparation and Revision

An evolving, **company-neutral**, senior-level Go learning and interview-revision library. The goal is to revise concepts confidently before future interviews, rather than restarting preparation each time.

These first seven modules are adapted from the supplied long-form interview-preparation transcript. The order is **topic order (1–N)**, not a daily schedule. Add new topics as `8-<topic>`, `9-<topic>`, and so on; keep existing paths stable so bookmarks continue to work.

## How to revise effectively

1. **Start with recall.** Try answering the module's [revision checkpoint](#module-contents-and-topic-links) questions aloud, before reading.
2. **Read only the gaps.** Follow the index below to the concept, example, Q&A, or practical exercise you missed.
3. **Prove it.** Run/modify examples, use `go test -race`, benchmarks or profiles where appropriate, and distinguish observed results from assumptions.
4. **Track uncertainty.** Record what you cannot explain in that module's `NOTES.md`, and capture your confidence and next review date in [REVISION-LOG.md](REVISION-LOG.md).
5. **Prepare for a new interview.** Prioritize low-confidence concepts and recent mistakes; reuse completed explanations instead of learning from scratch.

**Confidence scale:** `1` = unfamiliar · `2` = recognize but cannot explain · `3` = explain with notes · `4` = explain without notes and solve examples · `5` = defend design trade-offs and diagnose failures.
